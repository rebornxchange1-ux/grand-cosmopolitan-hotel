Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check in and check out?

Check in begins at 3:00 PM and check out is by 12:00 PM. Early check in or late check out may be arranged through our reservations team, subject to availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms vary by rate and season. Our reservations team will confirm the exact policy for your dates during your chat with us.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast availability depends on the room package selected. Our reservations team will confirm this when finalizing your booking.

Do you offer airport transfer?

Yes, airport transfer can be arranged upon request. Please mention this to our reservations team during your chat.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes, complimentary parking is available for all hotel guests.

Are pets allowed?

Please contact our reservations team directly to discuss our pet policy ahead of your stay.

How do I make a reservation?

Simply select your dates and room type above and click "Check Availability." This opens a live chat with our reservations team, who will personally confirm and finalize your booking.
